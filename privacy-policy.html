Privacy Policy

Kids Learning App • Last Revised: February 21, 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to Kids Learning App. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains that we do not collect, capture, store, or transmit any personal data or personally identifiable information on external servers while you or your child use our Kids Learning App.

2. Local On-Device Data Storage

To provide a personalized user interface and save your child's learning progress across app sessions, the app stores minimal preference data (such as profile name, selected avatar, unlocked level stages, and total stars earned) strictly locally on your device using device local storage (SharedPreferences). This data stays 100% offline on your device, is never transmitted or uploaded to any remote server or third party, and can be cleared at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

3. Information Sharing & Third-Party Access

We do not collect, track, or share any personal information, location data, or device identifiers. No user data is ever sold, disclosed, or shared with third parties or external services.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy Compliance)

Our Kids Learning App is built specifically for children. We fully comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy. We do not require account creation or registration, nor do we request, collect, or track personal information from children under any circumstances.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal or regulatory obligations. Any updates will be published directly on this page.
